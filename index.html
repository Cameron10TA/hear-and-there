    HEAR & THERE

    A walk with
    a little more wonder.

    1. Let us find your place.

      Allow location access to use the live walk or simulation. If access is denied, the experience stays locked.

    2. Tap, then wander.

      Tap Start exploring to enable sound. Near a landmark, an arrival sound and short story play automatically. You can also tap any map marker or place card to listen. Choose Follow nearby stories when you’re ready to return to automatic arrivals.

    3. Stay in the moment.

      Keep this page open and your screen on. Locking the screen or switching away pauses your walk. Tap Resume when you return.

    Explore freely or collect all 56 stories. A finished narration earns a Listened badge; opening a card does not. Your collection clears when you end the walk, reload, or revoke location access. Look for the hidden thread beneath the place list for a smaller challenge.

    We use location only in memory during this session. No accounts, analytics, cookies, or saved preferences. Your browser may remember its permission choice and cache site files. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap, which can see the map area and your IP address; your precise GPS coordinates are not sent by this app.
