# Content and asset sources

Original sources checked October 15, 2026; new stops and footprint data checked October 20, 2026. Narrations are short original summaries of these sources, mixed with observation prompts. They are not recordings of students or real ambient sound.

## Listening footprints

Court and building outlines come from the polygon geometry in [Whitman’s interactive map](https://www.whitman.edu/campus-map?raw=true), using the place IDs linked above. Longitude/latitude pairs were converted to latitude/longitude and rounded to seven decimal places. The physical footprint is retained for distance and overlap decisions. A separate outdoor listening polygon now extends to the nearby mapped paths. The 8 m entry and 14 m exit margins apply outside that walking area; they are prototype settings, not survey data or permission to enter a building. Styx retains its circular zone. All sides and GPS behavior still need an on-campus field check. The five residence hall boundaries were also updated after integrating the teammate’s stops; their content and recordings are unchanged.

## Complete campus building inventory (October 27, 2026)

The [official Whitman campus map](https://www.whitman.edu/campus-map?raw=true), retrieved October 27, 2026, lists 54 building features in the Walla Walla campus area. All 54 now have stories, alongside Styx and the outdoor courts, for 56 stops. This includes the Climbing Center as a separate named map feature adjoining Sherwood Athletic Center. Bracher Observatory (x37167, Erickson Farm north of town) and Pacific Northwest Regional Observatory (x37197, Braden Farm near Wallula Gap) are remote research sites, outside this campus walk and its map bounds. Unlisted rental houses, unnamed sheds, and individual structures within a named building complex are not claimed as separate stops.

The 45 additions below use short original summaries of each official map entry, with observation prompts written for an outdoor listener. No hours, interior access, or current fraternity activity are promised. Wellness House's map entry still includes legacy Global Awareness House wording; the new story uses its mapped name and residential role without repeating that conflicting program description. New recordings are synthetic samples made locally with **Microsoft Zira Desktop**, Windows System.Speech, mono 16-bit PCM WAV at 22,050 Hz. The exact spoken text is the corresponding transcript in src/landmarks.js. Existing audio and transcripts are preserved; approximate duration labels now reflect the actual WAV durations.

Physical outlines are the official map polygon rings, converted from longitude/latitude to latitude/longitude, rounded to seven decimals, with duplicate closing/adjacent vertices removed. Marker points lie inside the actual footprints. Separate listening polygons enclose the footprint and selected nearby outdoor approaches. The entry/exit margins remain 8/14 m. Listening polygons can include recessed exterior space; the physical outlines keep their concavities for distance and overlap selection.

Path evidence comes from this [bounded OSM development-time extract](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=-118.3355,46.0674,-118.3228,46.0762), retrieved October 27, 2026. Only footways, pedestrian paths, and the Bike Shop's mapped service alley were selected; ways marked private/no foot access were excluded. Mapped roads, fences, waterways, and neighboring footprints were checked when fitting approaches. The Bike Shop story is heard from the alley outside the gate; the photograph shows a fence, not an invitation to enter. No new runtime source or tile-prefetch request is added. OSM-derived geometry is © OpenStreetMap contributors under [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Raw extracts and review plots stay in ignored local review files.

**Estimated frontages:** College House, Lavender House, Sherwood House, and Third Space Center lack a suitable separately mapped same-side sidewalk in this extract. Their listening points use a provisional line 6 m from the mapped street centerline, on the building's side, near the building frontage. This is an estimate of an outdoor street-side listening area, not surveyed sidewalk geometry or proof of public access. Official photos were inspected for building identity and visible exterior approaches; they do not establish exact sidewalk positions or present access. Confirm the actual public sidewalk and adjust the outline on site. Sherwood House must be heard from the public street frontage, without entering its private stairs, driveway, or grounds. Third Space's south path is nearer Fouts; the provisional west frontage keeps its own arrival distinct. These four cases have separately marked model checkpoints, rather than being described as OSM sidewalk observations.

### Adjacent-stop selection and demo points

The existing nearest-physical-footprint and current-entry/exit behavior is retained. New buildings now participate in previously two-stop paths: Olin/Jewett, Maxey/courts, Hall of Science/Penrose, Harper Joy/Penrose, Climbing Center/Memorial, Hunter/Reid, Prentiss/Douglas, and Writing House/Cleveland. The regression tests explicitly name the nearer stop on these shared source points, while separately retaining coverage by each building's own listening area. Beta Theta Pi/Olin and Writing House/Cleveland have unavoidable shared-sidewalk exit overlap: a current story can remain selected there until departure, while fresh arrival selects the nearer footprint. The complete demo approaches them after leaving their neighbor.

Existing listening positions were moved to avoid new neighbors: Outdoor Tennis Courts: 46.0708577, -118.3286126, [way 771170320](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/771170320); Penrose Library: 46.0712926, -118.3294756, [way 1565062337](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1565062337); Cleveland Commons: 46.0683301, -118.3285186, [way 1296430168](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1296430168); Jewett Hall: 46.0728285, -118.3268509, [way 1565339196](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1565339196). Maxey's point uses [way 138193998](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/138193998) at 46.0715497, -118.3271904. These source points are outside the footprints.

The demo is a 50:48 presentation with outdoor listening holds and waypoint transitions, not walking directions. Every interpolated tenth-second position is checked for exactly one arrival per stop in the intended order. Listening holds allow at least three seconds beyond each decoded recording's duration. Four provisional street frontages and physical iPhone/GPS behavior still require the field checks in IPHONE-TESTING.md.
