# Content and asset sources

Sources checked October 20, 2026. Narrations are short original summaries of these sources, mixed with observation prompts. They are not recordings of students or real ambient sound.

## Styx

- Facts: [Whitman campus sculpture tour](https://www.whitman.edu/documents/global/Campus-Tours/Whitman-Sculpture-Tour.pdf), entry 22, describes Deborah Butterfield, 2002, bronze cast from driftwood, the Snake and Columbia rivers, and fabrication at the Walla Walla Foundry.
- Position: [OpenStreetMap node 12023094079](https://www.openstreetmap.org/node/12023094079), 46.0720452, -118.3290799. OpenStreetMap data is © OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL; verify the GPS trigger on campus.
- Photograph: [Whitman campus-map photograph](https://www.whitman.edu/images/about/map/Map/55styxinautumn-ankeny.jpg). Downloaded unchanged; credit Whitman College. No open license is asserted.

## Outdoor tennis courts

- Location and description: [Whitman interactive campus map](https://www.whitman.edu/campus-map?plc=x37212). Located between Memorial Building and Maxey Hall. Coordinate chosen within the official map's outdoor-court polygon, 46.07137, -118.32851. The site says practices, classes, and students have priority.
- Photograph: [Whitman campus-map photograph](https://www.whitman.edu/images/about/map/Map/tennis.jpg). Downloaded unchanged; credit Whitman College. No open license is asserted.

## First-year residence halls

- Facts: [Whitman Residence Halls](https://www.whitman.edu/life-at-whitman/residence-life/residence-halls), including the first-year communities, Jewett Café, Ankeny Field, and Stanton Field context.
- Names: [Whitman Editorial Style Guide](https://www.whitman.edu/communications/writing-and-editing/editorial-style-guide) uses **Jewett Hall**, **Lyman House**, and **Anderson Hall**.
- Positions: Whitman interactive campus-map polygons for [Jewett Hall](https://www.whitman.edu/campus-map?plc=x37184), [Lyman House](https://www.whitman.edu/campus-map?plc=x37188), and [Anderson Hall](https://www.whitman.edu/campus-map?plc=x37158). Triggers are placed just outside the polygons on the exterior paths at 46.07282, -118.32744; 46.07224, -118.32623; and 46.06896, -118.33014, respectively. Field-test the circles on campus.

## Sophomore residence halls

- Facts: [Whitman Residence Halls](https://www.whitman.edu/life-at-whitman/residence-life/residence-halls) describes Stanton Hall as housing mostly sophomores and describes Douglas Hall's suites and shared courtyard. The [Whitman campus map](https://www.whitman.edu/campus-map?plc=x37174) identifies Douglas Hall as named for William O. Douglas, Whitman Class of 1920.
- Positions: Whitman interactive campus-map polygons for [Stanton Hall](https://www.whitman.edu/campus-map?plc=x46557) and [Douglas Hall](https://www.whitman.edu/campus-map?plc=x37174). Trigger points are placed about 20 m north-east of Stanton and 22 m south of Douglas, toward their exterior grounds at 46.06887, -118.32945 and 46.06946, -118.32885. Field-test the circles on campus.
- Photographs: [Stanton Hall](https://www.whitman.edu/images/life-at-whitman/residence-life/Halls/stanton-hall-exterior-volleyball.jpg) and [Douglas Hall](https://www.whitman.edu/images/life-at-whitman/residence-life/Halls/douglas-hall-exterior.jpg), provided by Whitman College. Downloaded unchanged; no open license is asserted.

## Design, map, and audio

- Colors: [Whitman brand guide](https://brand.whitman.edu/), blue #002868 and yellow #FFC627. This project uses a custom wordmark, Georgia/system fonts, and no official seal or restricted college logo.
- Map: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), rendered with [Leaflet 1.9.4](https://leafletjs.com/). The Leaflet distribution and license are vendored locally.
- Sample narration: Styx and tennis were synthesized locally with Microsoft Zira Desktop using Windows System.Speech. Penrose, Cleveland, Jewett, Lyman, Anderson, Stanton, and Douglas were synthesized locally with macOS Samantha. All are mono PCM WAV at 22,050 Hz. Replace these samples with team recordings when ready. Each transcript is in `src/landmarks.js`.
- Arrival sounds: original sine-wave chimes and bouncing tones generated by `src/audio.js`. These are sound design, not recordings of the actual locations.
- Icon: original SVG waveform created for this project.
