# Content and asset sources

Original sources checked October 15, 2026; new stops and footprint data checked October 20, 2026. Narrations are short original summaries of these sources, mixed with observation prompts. They are not recordings of students or real ambient sound.

## Listening footprints

Court and building outlines come from the polygon geometry in [Whitman’s interactive map](https://www.whitman.edu/campus-map?raw=true), using the place IDs linked above. Longitude/latitude pairs were converted to latitude/longitude and rounded to seven decimal places. The physical footprint is retained for distance and overlap decisions. A separate outdoor listening polygon now extends to the nearby mapped paths. The 8 m entry and 14 m exit margins apply outside that walking area; they are prototype settings, not survey data or permission to enter a building. Styx retains its circular zone. All sides and GPS behavior still need an on-campus field check. The five residence hall boundaries were also updated after integrating the teammate’s stops; their content and recordings are unchanged.

## Design, map, and audio

Outdoor walking outlines and demo listening positions were fitted on October 20, 2026 to OpenStreetMap footways retrieved through the [OSM map API](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=-118.3324,46.0680,-118.3277,46.0725). This is a development-time source; the app makes no path-data requests. OpenStreetMap data is © OpenStreetMap contributors, licensed under [ODbL](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The outlines are approximate listening areas, not surveyed access boundaries or walking directions. Reference ways include:

- Courts: [847806503](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/847806503), [771170320](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/771170320), and [771170906](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/771170906).

- Memorial: [771170906](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/771170906), [771170911](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/771170911), and [847807105](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/847807105).
- Cleveland: [1565067586](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1565067586), [1565091975](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1565091975), [1296430169](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1296430169), and [1565095479](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1565095479).
- Reid: [1565062347](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1565062347), [1446463280](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1446463280), and [1446463287](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1446463287).

- Colors: [Whitman brand guide](https://brand.whitman.edu/), blue #002868 and yellow #FFC627. This project uses a custom wordmark, Georgia/system fonts, and no official seal or restricted college logo.
- Map: [OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), rendered with [Leaflet 1.9.4](https://leafletjs.com/). The Leaflet distribution and license are vendored locally.
- Sample narration: Styx, tennis, Memorial, and Reid were synthesized locally with Microsoft Zira Desktop using Windows System.Speech. Penrose, Cleveland, Jewett, Lyman, Anderson, Stanton, and Douglas were synthesized locally with macOS Samantha. All are mono PCM WAV at 22,050 Hz. Replace these samples with team recordings when ready. Each transcript is in `src/landmarks.js`.
- Arrival sounds: original sine-wave chimes and bouncing tones generated by `src/audio.js`. These are sound design, not recordings of the actual locations.
- Icon: original SVG waveform created for this project.

### Residence hall path boundaries (October 20, 2026)

The southern paths use the October 20 extract above. Jewett/Lyman paths use the [northern OSM extract](https://api.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=-118.3300,46.0705,-118.3250,46.0735) retrieved October 13, 2026 and inspected again October 20. A fresh expanded extract was rate-limited; these are the actual source dates, not a claim of a new survey. Physical outlines come from the Whitman map retrieved October 20.

- Jewett: ways [1565063616](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1565063616), 1565095483, 155689352, and 1565339196 cover the north sidewalk, west approach, and recessed south approach.
- Lyman: [155689352](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/155689352) follows the west side; 138193998 reaches the south/southeast approach. The eastern wall has perimeter coverage, but the extract does not establish an accessible path along its full length. Verify access on site.
- Anderson: [1296430169](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1296430169), 1565059645, 1565067569, and 1565063625 cover west, north/recess, east, and south paths.
- Stanton: [1565062349](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1565062349), 1296430168, and 1296430169 cover its west sidewalk and south/east/north approaches.
- Douglas: [1565090820](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1565090820), 1565067615, 1565062350, and 1567226416 cover the north/east approaches and west/south paths. East/south outline corners bridge back to the physical perimeter rather than extending to the distant street.

Walking polygons enclose the footprint and selected adjacent path points, including recessed exterior space; they do not represent permission to enter residence grounds. The 8/14 m margins remain starting values. Building markers are separate from outdoor listening positions. Jewett/Lyman’s physical connection and Anderson/Cleveland’s shared path necessarily have narrow overlaps: fresh arrival chooses the nearer footprint, while a current story remains selected through small drift until its entry area is left. Only one story is active. See the regression checkpoints in `tests/proximity.test.js` and field checklist. OSM-derived geometry is © OpenStreetMap contributors under ODbL.
